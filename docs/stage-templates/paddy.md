# Paddy stage template (seed source)

Transplanted, medium-duration (~120 day) variety. Day ranges are approximate and editable per variety. General guidance only — confirm with the local KVK / agriculture officer. Each line becomes a checklist item (type: do / watch / save).

## 1. Land preparation (3–4 weeks before transplanting)
- do: Summer ploughing
- do: Sow and incorporate green manure (daincha / sunhemp) at flowering
- do: Apply FYM / compost
- do: Puddle and level well; repair bunds
- watch: Uneven levelling — patchy water, more weeds
- save: Green manure + FYM replace bought nutrients; good levelling cuts water and weeding cost

## 2. Nursery (~20–25 days)
- do: Use certified or own selected seed; salt-water test
- do: Treat seed with Pseudomonas / Azospirillum
- do: Raise nursery near the water source
- watch: Over-aged seedlings (>25 days); nursery pests
- save: Lower seed rate (SRI / mat nursery); keep own seed for next season

## 3. Transplanting (day 0 in main field)
- do: 2–3 seedlings per hill at proper spacing (single young seedlings for SRI)
- do: Keep shallow water 2–3 cm
- do: Fill gaps within 7–10 days
- watch: Labour shortage delaying planting; planting too deep
- save: Compare manual vs machine transplanting cost per acre

## 4. Tillering / vegetative (~15–45 days after transplanting)
- do: Weed at ~15 and ~30 days (cono / rotary weeder)
- do: Apply Jeevamirtham through irrigation water
- do: Grow Azolla
- do: Alternate wetting and drying
- watch: Stem borer, leaf folder, early weed flush — set pheromone and light traps
- save: Timely weeding is cheaper than late hand weeding; AWD cuts pumping cost

## 5. Panicle initiation to booting (~45–65 days)
- do: No water stress
- do: Foliar Panchagavyam
- watch: Brown planthopper, sheath blight (dense crop, excess nitrogen)
- save: Avoid excess nitrogen; spray only above pest threshold

## 6. Flowering (~65–80 days)
- do: Keep ~5 cm water
- do: Avoid spraying during morning flowering hours
- watch: Neck blast, false smut, ear-head bug
- save: Scout twice a week to avoid panic spraying

## 7. Grain filling and maturity (~80–110 days)
- do: Drain field 10–15 days before harvest
- do: Protect from rats and birds
- watch: Lodging, rat damage, grain discolouration
- save: Early drainage allows cheaper machine harvesting

## 8. Harvest and post-harvest
- do: Harvest at ~80% straw-coloured grains
- do: Dry to 12–14% moisture
- do: Clean bags and storage
- watch: Moisture cuts at the mill; storage pests
- save: Proper drying avoids price cuts; book harvester early; sell straw and record it as income
