# Paddy checklist by stage (seed source)

Uses the default stage list. Transplanted, medium-duration (~120 day) variety; day ranges approximate. General guidance only — confirm with the local KVK / agriculture officer. Each line becomes a checklist item (type: do / watch / save).

## 1. Preparation (3–4 weeks before transplanting)
- do: Summer ploughing
- do: Sow and incorporate green manure (daincha / sunhemp) at flowering
- do: Apply FYM / compost
- do: Puddle and level well; repair bunds
- watch: Uneven levelling — patchy water, more weeds
- save: Green manure + FYM replace bought nutrients; good levelling cuts water and weeding cost

## 2. Seeding — nursery (~20–25 days)
- do: Use certified or own selected seed; salt-water test
- do: Treat seed with Pseudomonas / Azospirillum
- do: Raise nursery near the water source
- watch: Over-aged seedlings (>25 days); nursery pests
- save: Lower seed rate (SRI / mat nursery); keep own seed for next season

## 3. Transplanting (day 0 in main field)
- do: 2–3 seedlings per hill at proper spacing (single young seedlings for SRI)
- do: Keep shallow water 2–3 cm; fill gaps within 7–10 days
- watch: Labour shortage delaying planting; planting too deep
- save: Compare manual vs machine transplanting cost per acre

## 4. Growing — tillering to booting (~15–65 days after transplanting)
- do: Weed at ~15 and ~30 days (cono / rotary weeder)
- do: Apply Jeevamirtham through irrigation water; grow Azolla
- do: Alternate wetting and drying; no water stress from panicle initiation
- do: Foliar Panchagavyam at panicle initiation
- watch: Stem borer, leaf folder, brown planthopper, sheath blight — set pheromone and light traps
- save: Timely weeding is cheaper than late hand weeding; avoid excess nitrogen; spray only above pest threshold

## 5. Fruiting — flowering and grain filling (~65–110 days)
- do: Keep ~5 cm water at flowering; avoid spraying during morning flowering hours
- do: Drain field 10–15 days before harvest; protect from rats and birds
- watch: Neck blast, false smut, ear-head bug, lodging
- save: Scout twice a week to avoid panic spraying; early drainage allows cheaper machine harvesting

## 6. Harvesting
- do: Harvest at ~80% straw-coloured grains; book harvester early
- watch: Grain losses from delayed harvest
- save: Machine harvest vs manual — compare cost per acre

## 7. Post Harvest
- do: Dry to 12–14% moisture; clean bags and storage
- watch: Storage pests; moisture build-up
- save: Proper drying avoids moisture cuts at the mill

## 8. Sold
- do: Compare rates (mill, DPC, trader) before selling; record deductions
- watch: Moisture cuts, commission, transport and hamali charges
- save: Sell straw separately and record it as income
